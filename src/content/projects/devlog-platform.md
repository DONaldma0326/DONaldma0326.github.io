## Overview

Devlog Platform helps developers publish short technical updates quickly. The goal was to reduce writing friction while keeping the content structure clean.

## What I built

- Markdown-first editor and publishing flow
- Topic tags and filtering for easier discovery
- Lightweight analytics dashboard for post activity

## Key result

Publishing time per post dropped from around 30 minutes to under 10 minutes.
