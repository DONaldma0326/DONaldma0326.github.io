## 🎯 Introduction
I have officially registered for the **AWS Certified Data Engineer – Associate** exam, which I will be taking in July 2026. In these series of post, I will be documenting my preparation process and sharing the resources I'm using to study.

To start, I highly recommend reviewing the [official AWS study guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/data-engineer-associate-01/data-engineer-associate-01.html). Understanding the exam guidelines and question formats is the best way to get started.

## 📚 Study Resources
Personally, I am focusing on utilizing free resources to prepare:
* [**AWS Skill Builder**](https://skillbuilder.aws/learning-plan/QYZWVSMX4B/exam-prep-plan-aws-certified-data-engineer--associate-deac01--english/YTMBK7R698): The official exam prep plan.
* [**Coursera**](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/aws-data-engineer-certification): Supplemental course material.
* **YouTube:** For visual learning and architectural deep dives.

## 🚀 My Preparation Strategy
My core strategy is to follow the **[AWS Certified Data Engineer – Associate (DEA-C01) Full Course (285 min)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6G0bLDIcO7Y)** by Johnny Chivers (Thank you for making this video). I am supplementing this by reading official AWS documentation on data solutions as I progress through the course.

To practice, I am using:
* [**ExamTopics**](https://www.examtopics.com/exams/amazon/aws-certified-data-engineer-associate-dea-c01/view/1/): For realistic practice questions.
* [**AWS Skill Builder Official Practice Set**](https://skillbuilder.aws/learn/2JS5H1Z9KP/official-practice-question-set-aws-certified-data-engineer--associate-deac01--english/VX268Y5VBA?parentId=YTMBK7R698): To get familiar with the official testing environment and logic.

Follow along on my journey, and wish me luck! ✨
