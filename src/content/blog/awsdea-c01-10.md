## Amazon QuickSight

Amazon QuickSight is a managed business intelligence (BI) service. Users can easily create and publish interactive dashboards accessible to both data analysts and management. QuickSight is notable for its scalability and ease of use.

QuickSight is commonly used for business reporting, data exploration, operational analytics, and customer insights.


## Data Integration

QuickSight integrates well with other AWS data infrastructure such as Redshift, RDS, Athena, and S3. It also supports on-premises databases, a wide range of SaaS applications, cloud databases (e.g., Snowflake), and flat file uploads.

## Accessing QuickSight from a VPC

A common exam scenario is how to access QuickSight within a VPC. The following steps are required:

1. Create a VPC endpoint for QuickSight.
2. Configure the security group.
3. Enable VPC access in QuickSight.
